F1Q Plane Launch Process

F1Q's are electric-powered (not remote controlled) free flight planes. They require meticulous setup and processes to master

Free Flight Model Retrival

Since free flight planes are not controlled in the air, they often fly far away due to the wind, which leads competitors to walk many, many kilometers to retrieve our planes.

Aeromodelling Competitions

There are four main Class-F1 plane types. F1A: Glider (most traditional). F1B: Rubber. F1P/F1C Gas/Combustion (quite dangerous). F1Q: Electric (flown for the first time at the world championships in 2026).
